# Google Form: BNI-ECR Network membership

Intro text: "Membership is free. We use your answers only to run the network (emails, meeting invitations and, if you agree, a member profile). You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time."

1. Full name (short answer, required)
2. Email address (short answer, required)
3. Institution (short answer, required)
4. Country (short answer, required)
5. Current career stage (multiple choice: PhD candidate / Postdoctoral researcher / Within ~5 years of completing PhD / Other)
6. Main research topic (short answer, required)
7. Crop, plant or agricultural system studied (short answer)
8. Methods or areas of expertise (paragraph)
9. Your connection to BNI, in a few sentences (paragraph, required)
10. I am interested in (checkboxes: presenting a mini-webinar / contributing to joint writing / helping organize activities)
11. May we list you in the public member directory on the website? (multiple choice: Yes / No, not yet)
12. I agree that my details are stored for running the network (checkbox, required)
