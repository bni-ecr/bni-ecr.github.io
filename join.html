Join the Network

Membership is free and open to eligible researchers internationally.

Who it is for

  • PhD candidates
  • Postdoctoral researchers
  • Researchers within approximately five years of completing their PhD
  • Researchers whose work is directly connected to BNI or its application

Not sure whether you qualify? Just ask. We are happy to talk.

What we will ask

  • Full name
  • Institution
  • Country
  • Current career stage
  • Main research topic
  • Crop, plant or agricultural system studied
  • Methods or areas of expertise
  • Email address
  • A short explanation of your connection to BNI
  • Whether you are interested in presenting a mini-webinar, contributing to joint writing or helping organize activities

We use your answers only to run the network (welcome emails, meeting invitations and, if you agree, a member profile). You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time by writing to .
