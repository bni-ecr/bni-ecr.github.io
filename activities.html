Activities

Ways to share, learn and collaborate with other early-career BNI researchers.

01

Network meetings

Regular online meetings, planned roughly every two months at rotating times to suit different time zones. Members share research updates, discuss common challenges and identify opportunities for collaboration.

02

Mini-webinars

Short scientific presentations by network members or invited researchers, followed by informal discussion.

03

Research exchange

Sharing of protocols, methodological experiences, relevant publications, research opportunities and practical lessons from BNI experiments.

04

Joint writing

Development of collaborative reviews, perspective articles, opinion pieces, conference contributions and research proposals.

05

Conference activities

Organization of early-career sessions, poster activities, networking opportunities and informal gatherings at international BNI meetings.

06

Member connections

Helping researchers find others working on similar crops, compounds, microbial groups, methods or agricultural systems.

Shape what we do

The network is community-driven. Members can offer a mini-webinar, propose a writing project, suggest a topic for discussion or help organize an activity. Standard methods and reproducibility were raised repeatedly at the kick-off meeting, and we would like to work on them together.
