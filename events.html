About the Tsukuba session

BNI Frontiers: Early-Career Perspectives gives early-career researchers an opportunity to present their work, meet other BNI researchers and discuss future collaborations. It is chaired by Gabriel S. Rocha and Guntur V. Subbarao as part of the 6th International BNI Consortium Meeting. Programme subject to updates.
